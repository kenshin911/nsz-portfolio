Naturasi Cheese Co. · Silang, Cavite

Naturasì Brand & Wholesale Website

A marketing and product website designed and built for a premier cheese manufacturer in Cavite, spotlighting authentic Italian-Filipino craftsmanship, restaurant distribution, and wholesale lead generation.

An independent website concept and proposal built for Naturasì Cheese Co. The website was not commissioned or deployed.

  • RoleUI/UX & Frontend build
  • StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • TypeBrand & wholesale website

The opportunity

Naturasì crafts exceptional small-batch Italian cheeses (Burrata, Stracciatella, Provola, and Mozzarella) in Cavite, trusted by top Manila restaurants and featured in Inquirer Lifestyle. However, their online footprint was fragmented across social media, with no centralized catalog for wholesale buyers, no interactive distributor guide for food lovers, and no structured inquiry funnel for restaurant partnerships.

What I designed & built

I built a custom, lightweight, mobile-first brand website that honors their heritage while driving business growth. The site features an artisan cheese catalog with tasting notes, a 4-step farm-to-table cheesemaking walkthrough, verified press features, a searchable distributor directory, and a dedicated B2B wholesale capture funnel with direct WhatsApp routing.

Key website features

Artisan product catalog

Dedicated product cards with tasting profiles, pairing notes, and freshness tags for Burrata, Stracciatella, Provola, and Mozzarella varieties.

Farm-to-table process

A 4-step interactive storytelling module detailing high-quality milk sourcing, precise curdling, artisan stretching, and quality testing.

Wholesale lead capture

A structured B2B inquiry form categorized for wholesale accounts, retail distribution, and partnerships with fast WhatsApp quote routing.

Distributor & store search

An interactive directory helping customers and chefs find partner retail locations and grocery distributors across Metro Manila.

Press & editorial proof

Integrated coverage from Inquirer Lifestyle (“The Cheesemaker and His Burratas”) establishing immediate authority and credibility.

Interactive FAQ

An accessible accordion answering essential questions on shelf life, storage, wholesale volume tiers, and plant visits.

Design & technical strengths

Zero template bloat

Hand-coded with semantic HTML, modern CSS, and JavaScript, delivering instant mobile load times without heavy CMS baggage.

B2B conversion focus

Clear, direct calls-to-action placed strategically across the page so restaurant chefs and retailers can request pricing in seconds.

Crafted visual identity

Editorial typography, warm natural color palette, and structured hierarchy reflecting authentic Italian craftsmanship and Cavite heritage.

The story behind this build

I designed and built this website as a proactive pitch and complete brand proposal for Naturasì Cheese Co. alongside their internal plant system (NaturasiMIS). While the company owner did not move forward with taking it live, the entire build is fully coded, functional, and serves as a direct demonstration of how I design and engineer commercial websites for local producers and service businesses.
