Naturasi Cheese Co. · Silang, Cavite

NaturasiMIS

A capstone project built around Naturasì Cheese Co.'s real production workflow. Designed and developed as a browser-based management system to replace handwritten logs with live inventory tracking, batch management, production orders, and predictive restocking.

This system was built and tested as a capstone project — not deployed for company use. No public login exists. This walkthrough covers what was built: screenshots from the actual system, plus the problems it was designed to solve.

  • RoleDesign & engineering
  • StackLaravel, PHP, MySQL
  • TypeCapstone project · desktop-first

The problem

Naturasi is a cheese manufacturer in Silang, Cavite. Daily work (ingredients, batches, expiry, and production notes) lived in paper logs and Excel. That breaks down as soon as you have more than one cheese line: numbers drift, expiry gets missed, and nobody can see what is actually in production without walking the floor.

What I built

NaturasiMIS is a browser-based management system designed for the plant workflow. Staff would record stock and batches once; managers could see the same numbers across the system. Confirming an order was designed to deduct ingredients from inventory automatically, so production and storage would stay in sync instead of being maintained in separate notebooks.

Inside the system

Inventory

Inbound, outbound, ending stock, low-stock and out-of-stock flags, plus a predictive restock view so materials don’t silently run out.

Production batches

Batch numbers, product type, quantity, dates, status, and assigned staff, so every wheel of cheese can be traced after it leaves the vat.

Orders

Client POs with confirm / complete / cancel states. Confirming an order deducts ingredients from inventory instead of hoping someone updated the sheet.

Reports

Inventory and production summaries, best-selling vs slow-moving products, and stock charts management can actually use in a meeting.

Roles & logs

Admin, manager, inventory, and production accounts. Activity logs with CSV/PDF export so changes are accountable, not anonymous.

Plant settings

Company branding, backups, and restore: the unglamorous essentials a real factory system requires.

What changed on the floor

One set of numbers

Stock, batches, and orders live in the same system. Floor staff enter once; managers see the same inventory without waiting on a spreadsheet.

Orders that move stock

Confirming a client PO deducts ingredients automatically. Burrata and Stracciatella orders are no longer a separate notebook from the vat.

Who did what

Admin, manager, inventory, and production roles, plus activity logs. Changes are attributable, not “someone edited the sheet.”
