Privacy Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026 · nsz

This site is a freelance portfolio. I collect as little information as possible, and I do not sell it.

What I collect

  • Contact form details you choose to send: name, email, business type, and your message. These open in my email app so I can reply about a project.
  • Theme preference (light or night mode) stored in your browser with localStorage. That stays on your device and is not sent to me.

What I do not collect

  • No advertising cookies
  • No third-party analytics on this site
  • No payment data on this site (invoices are handled separately if we work together)

How I use it

I use inquiry details only to respond to you, quote a project, and keep a record of what we discussed. I do not share your information with marketers.

How long I keep it

Emails stay in my inbox for as long as needed to run the project or follow up. You can ask me to delete a conversation.

Your choices

You can email me to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser to reset night mode.
