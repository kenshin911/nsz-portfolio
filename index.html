How it works

A short, honest process. No mystery weeks.

  1. Talk it through

    You tell me what the business does, who should call, and what’s broken about the current site or lack of one.

  2. Design the pages

    I map the structure, write a tight visual direction, and show you layouts before anything is locked in.

  3. Build & polish

    I code it myself: fast, mobile-first, and easy to update. You get a site you can actually send people to.

  4. Launch

    We go live, I walk you through how to use it, and we tweak the last 10% that only shows up in the real world.

Questions

Before you email.

How long does a site take?

Most brochure sites land in 2–4 weeks, depending on how quickly we get photos, copy, and feedback. Systems like NaturasiMIS take longer because they are real software, not just pages.

Do I need to write the copy?

If you have it, great. If you don’t, I can draft clear page copy from a short call so you’re not staring at a blank page.

What do you need from me?

A few photos, your hours and services, and what you want people to do (call, book, visit, order). That’s enough to start.

Can you host and update it later?

Yes. We can talk hosting when we pick a plan. Small updates after launch are straightforward you’re not locked into a mystery CMS.
