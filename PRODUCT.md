# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** US and global small business owners, clinic directors, local contractors, trade professionals, and specialty food/retail shop owners.
- **Situation:** They have an outdated site or no web presence, losing potential local customers browsing on mobile devices. They want a professional online presence without dealing with expensive agency pitch decks, slow layers of account managers, or monthly retainer lock-in.
- **Jobs to be Done:**
  1. Look like an established, trustworthy business that customers feel confident contacting or booking.
  2. Provide friction-free customer paths (tap-to-call, service breakdowns, inquiry forms, location discovery).
  3. Replace messy manual operations with custom web tools when spreadsheets break down.

## Product Purpose

A focused portfolio and direct lead-generation surface for **nsz**, an independent web designer and developer. It exists to prove craft, demonstrate real operational capability through verified case studies, and convert visiting business owners into direct paying clients.

## Positioning

**"Simple sites. Built to get you customers, not awards."**

Unlike traditional agencies that charge thousands for bloated WordPress themes with monthly lock-in retainers, nsz delivers fast, bespoke, mobile-first websites and internal systems with flat-rate transparent pricing ($299 / $599), direct communication with the actual engineer, and 100% client code ownership.

## Operating Context

- **Client Interaction:** Direct email, asynchronous project updates, 2–4 week delivery timeline, 14 days of post-launch warranty support.
- **Technical Architecture:** Ultra-lightweight semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS3, lightweight JavaScript. Zero CMS maintenance overhead or security vulnerabilities for clients.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Live Lead Funnel:** Integrated Web3Forms serverless endpoint delivering customer inquiries directly to `naszjeighallensaguid@gmail.com` with mailto fallback.
- **Pricing Model:**
  - **Starter Tier ($299):** One-page high-converting landing page, mobile-first layout, launch in ~2 weeks.
  - **Business Tier ($599):** Multi-page small business website (up to 5 pages), services, about, contact, launch in 3–4 weeks.

## Product Principles

1. **Mobile-First by Default:** Over 70% of local customers browse on phones; navigation, readability, and tap targets are prioritized for small screens.
2. **Speed & Zero Bloat:** Clean hand-coded architecture ensures near-instant load times on cellular networks without heavy framework baggage.
3. **Radical Transparency:** Clear prices, explicit deliverables, and no hidden ongoing fees.
4. **Authentic Proof Over Fluff:** Demonstrate real systems and live websites rather than generic mockups.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA contrast standards.
- 44×44px minimum touch targets for all mobile interactive elements.
- Semantic HTML landmarks (`<main>`, `<header>`, `<footer>`, `<section>`) with skip navigation links.
